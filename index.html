Album Harbor

An independent macOS app for your personal OneDrive photo and video library.

Browse media and cloud albums, organize local collections, keep originals downloaded, and prepare edited photos or trimmed videos for export and sharing.

Requires macOS 13 or later and a personal Microsoft account. Work and school accounts are not supported by this release.

Read support instructions or review the privacy policy.

For help, use the public support issues. Do not post credentials, account details, signed URLs, private file names, recognized text, locations, or personal media.

Developed by Maximilian Bülowius. Independent of Microsoft and Apple.
